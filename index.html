How it works

A buyer locks native GEN against human-readable terms with dispute and final deadlines. Either party appends authenticated evidence, optionally citing a web source. The buyer can confirm delivery for an instant release, or anyone can trigger a permissionless timeout so funds never stick. On a dispute an LLM fetches each cited source and validators reach consensus via the Equivalence Principle; the losing party may stake a bond to appeal once, then a replay-safe, appeal-gated payout moves the held GEN.
