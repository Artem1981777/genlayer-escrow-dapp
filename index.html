How it works (v1). A buyer locks native GEN against human-readable terms with explicit dispute and final deadlines. Either party appends authenticated evidence, optionally citing a web source URL. The buyer can confirm delivery for an instant release, or anyone can trigger a permissionless timeout - release to the seller if undisputed, refund to the buyer past the final deadline - so funds are never stuck. On a dispute an LLM fetches each cited source and validators reach consensus via the Equivalence Principle; the loser may stake a bond to appeal once, then a replay-safe, appeal-gated payout transfers the held GEN.
Examples: 0x829DB851bc9963c71B22305e3b73bf5B220D1462 is a contested case resolved REFUND. Paste 0x679f4657d126Aa973A070E59654b6B8c37EaA7c0 for a fully-settled RELEASE (balance 0). The live v1 contract address will be added here after redeploy.
